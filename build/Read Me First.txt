DAWters and Son 2.3.4 for Mac (Apple Silicon)

INSTALL
1. Drag "DAWters and Son" onto the Applications folder.
2. Open it once from Applications. macOS will say it can't check the app
   for malicious software, because it isn't signed with an Apple developer ID.
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to DAWters and Son. Confirm with your password.
   (Alternative, in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/DAWters and Son.app")
4. The first time you record, allow microphone access.

GETTING STARTED
- Arm a track (red dot), press R to record, Space to stop.
- Tab steps through Deck, Tracks, Matrix, Pattern and Console.
- The Master tab (Shift+M) has 16 mastering recipes, A/B compare and Match target.
- View > Tuner (Shift+Cmd+T, or U) is a chromatic and instrument tuner.
- Edit > Noise removal takes a noise sample and cleans it out of clips.
- File > Export writes WAV, FLAC, MP3, AAC, Ogg or Opus; ffmpeg is built in.
- File > Save Song writes a .dawters file with all your audio inside.
- Your current song also autosaves inside the app.
- Settings (Cmd+,) picks your audio interface, calibrates recording latency,
  and switches between the Daytime and Late-show looks.
