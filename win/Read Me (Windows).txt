DAWters and Son 2.3.3 for Windows (64-bit)

INSTALL
1. Run DAWters-and-Son-2.3.3-Setup-x64.exe. It installs for your Windows account only,
   so no administrator password is needed.
2. Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC", because the installer
   isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Pick the shortcuts you want (desktop icon, D&S Tuner) and finish.

Prefer no installer? Unzip DAWters-and-Son-2.3.3-win-x64-portable.zip anywhere and run
"DAWters and Son.exe". (The portable copy doesn't register .dawters files.)

WHAT YOU GET
- DAWters and Son in the Start menu, and D&S Tuner, which opens straight into the tuner.
- Double-click a .dawters song to open it.
- ffmpeg is built in for MP3, AAC, Ogg, Opus and FLAC exports.
- Your songs, autosave and settings live in %APPDATA%\DAWters and Son.
- Uninstall from Settings > Apps.

AUDIO
For low latency use your audio interface's own driver, and pick it in Settings (Ctrl+,).
Windows audio goes through the system mixer (WASAPI shared mode), so the buffer setting
in Settings matters more than on a Mac.
