# Contributing to DAWters and Son

Thanks for helping. DAWters and Son is free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later, and it stays free:
no paid tiers, no sign-up and no tracking. Contributions must keep it that way: nothing that sends data
over the network by default, and nothing that only works for paying users.

- **Bugs:** open an issue with your platform (Mac, Windows, Ubuntu, Android or browser), the version
  from Help › About, and the steps to reproduce. A small `.dawters` song that shows the problem helps a lot.
- **Code:** `python3 build.py` builds `dist/dawters.html`; open it in Chrome to test. Keep the plain-JS,
  no-framework style, and add the SPDX header to new files. `sh build-all.sh` builds every package.
- **Translations, tutorials and songs** are just as welcome as code.

By contributing you agree that your work is licensed under the GPL-3.0-or-later.
